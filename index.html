Isochrone map of the blood arrival delays

JHU arterial territories (LVL1, 30 ROI) — group-average rapidtide delays, isochrone animation

The group-average bolus-arrival delays as a 3D isochrone animation: a bright wavefront (roughly a 0.25 s band) sweeps through the JHU arterial territories, while the tissue behind it keeps its own delay color on the coolwarm scale. The panel on the right lists the 30 regions in delay order — the earliest MCA territories around −0.5 s, the deep internal capsule last at about +0.9 s — and each row lights up in its own color as the front passes the region's mean delay. The time bar sits on the left, the color scale at the bottom.

Data: JHU ICBM-DTI-81 white-matter labels, group-mean rapidtide delays from the UK Biobank cohort.
